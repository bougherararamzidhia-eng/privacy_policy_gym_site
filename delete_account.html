Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from the Gym Owner App, Gym Staff App, or Gym Member App, please follow the instructions below.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can request account deletion directly within the application:

  1. Open the app and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to Settings or Profile.
  3. Scroll down to the "Danger Zone" or "Account Management" section.
  4. Tap on Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice. This action is irreversible.

Option 2: Email Request

If you cannot access the app, you may submit a deletion request via email:

  1. Compose an email to bougherararamzidhia.eng@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - [Your App Name].
  3. Include your registered email address and full name in the body of the email.
  4. We may ask for additional verification to prove ownership of the account.

Data Deletion & Retention Policy

What will be deleted?

  • Personal Identity Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile photos.
  • App Data: Gym configurations, member lists, attendance records, and workout plans associated with your account.
  • Authentication Credentials: Your login links and sessions will be invalidated and removed.

What will be retained?

  • Transaction Records: Financial transaction data may be retained for a period of up to 5 years (or as required by local law) for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Anonymized Analytics: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable data used for improving our service may be kept.

Requests are typically processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been permanently removed.
