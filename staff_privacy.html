Privacy Policy - Gym Staff App

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gym Management Ecosystem ("we", "us", or "our") collects and handles information when you use the Gym Staff Application as an employee or staff member of a gym utilizing our platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Provided by Your Employer

Your account is created and managed by the Gym Owner (your employer). They provide:

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, and potentially email address.
  • Employment Details: Role, shift timings, and permissions within the app.

1.2 Information Collected During Use

  • Activity Logs: To ensure security and accountability, we log actions performed within the app, such as checking in a member, recording a payment, or updating member details.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and identifiers to secure access.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

  • Authenticate your access to the gym management system.
  • Enable you to perform your duties (e.g., verify membership, record sales).
  • Create an audit trail for the Gym Owner to review daily operations.
  • Improve app performance and security.

3. Data Sharing and Control

3.1 Sharing with the Gym Owner

All data generated by your activity in the app is directly accessible to the Gym Owner. They are the "Data Controller" of this information. We act as the "Data Processor."

3.2 Third Parties

We may share anonymized usage data with service providers to improve infrastructure. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data Security

We use rigorous security protocols to protect your login credentials and activity data. Access is restricted based on the permissions set by the Gym Owner.

5. Your Rights

Since your data is processed on behalf of the Gym Owner:

  • Access & Correction: Please contact your gym management to update your personal details.
  • Account Deletion: Your account is managed by the Gym Owner; only they can deactivate or delete your staff profile.

6. Contact Us

For technical issues regarding the app, please contact:

Email: support@gymeco.com

For privacy inquiries related to your employment data, please contact your Gym Owner directly.
