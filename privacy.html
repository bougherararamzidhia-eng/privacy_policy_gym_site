Last Updated: January 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Gym Management Ecosystem ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you about how we handle your personal data when you use our mobile applications.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of data we collect include:

2.1 Information You Give Us

  • Identity Data: Name, Username, Date of Birth.
  • Contact Data: Email address, Phone number.
  • Profile Data: Profile photographs.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device Data: We collect Device IDs (UUID) to handle secure logins and attendance tracking (to prevent fraud).
  • Usage Data: Details of your gym visits (check-in/check-out times).

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To manage your gym membership, process payments, and track attendance.
  • Security: To verify your identity and prevent unauthorized access to gym facilities.
  • Communication: To send you notifications about your membership status or gym announcements.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Cloud Platform (Firestore & Firebase). We implement appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way.

5. Your Rights

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (Delete Account).

To request account deletion, please use the "Quit Job" or "Leave Gym" feature in the app, or contact your Gym Owner directly.
