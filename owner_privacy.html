Privacy Policy - Gym Owner App

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gym Management Ecosystem ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Gym Owner Application. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected and that we comply with all applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password, and phone number used for registration.
  • Business Information: Gym name, location, logo, business policies, and contact details.
  • Transaction Records: Details of cash payments received from members, recorded manually for accounting purposes. We do not collect or store bank account or credit card credentials for member payments.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the app, such as features accessed, time spent, and error logs.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.

1.3 Member and Staff Data

As a data controller using our platform, you input data regarding your members and staff. We process this data on your behalf as a data processor. This includes member names, contact info, attendance records, and payment history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Gym Owner App services.
  • To record and track your gym's financial transactions.
  • To generate analytics and reports regarding your gym's performance.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, security alerts, and support.
  • To improve our services based on usage patterns and feedback.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party vendors (e.g., cloud hosting) who assist in operating our services.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, or acquisition.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption, secure servers, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Export your data in a portable format.
