Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: February 21, 2026

At Gym Management Ecosystem (developed by Bougherara Ramzi Dhia), we respect your privacy and provide you with complete control over your personal data. This page outlines how you can request the deletion of your data from our systems.

Note: You can request the deletion of all your data (account closure) or specific parts of your data without deleting your account.

How to Request Deletion

Option 1: In-App (Automated)

The fastest way to delete your entire account and associated data:

  1. Open the Gym Owner, Gym Staff, or Gym Member app.
  2. Go to Settings or Profile.
  3. Select Account Management or Security.
  4. Tap Delete Account and follow the confirmation prompts.

Option 2: Email Request (Partial or Full)

Use this method if you want to delete specific data or cannot access the app:

  1. Send an email to bougherara.ramzidhia@univ_khenchela.com.
  2. Use the subject: Data Deletion Request - [Your Name/App].
  3. Clearly specify if you want to delete all data or specific records (e.g., "Delete my attendance history but keep my membership").
  4. Include your registered email address for verification.

Data Retention & Deletion Details

Below is a summary of the types of data we process and how deletion is handled:

Data Type Deletion Policy Retention Period
Account Profile (Name, Email, Phone) Permanently deleted upon request. Immediate (up to 30 days processing).
Attendance & Visit History Deleted upon request or account closure. Immediate (up to 30 days processing).
Payment & Transaction Records Aggregated/Anonymized for accounting. Up to 5 years (Legal/Tax requirement).
Gym Configurations (Owners) Permanently deleted upon account closure. Immediate (up to 30 days processing).
Technical Logs & Analytics Anonymized (non-identifiable). Up to 12 months.

Processing Time

Once a request is received via email, we will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete. In-app account deletion is typically processed immediately, though some data might remain in backups for a short period until overwritten.
