Privacy Policy - Gym Member App

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gym Management Ecosystem ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our Gym Member Application to interact with your gym facility.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Identity Data: Name, date of birth, and profile photograph.
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone number, and physical address.
  • Health Data (Optional): Height, weight, and fitness goals if you choose to input them for tracking purposes.

1.2 Usage and Transaction Data

  • Membership Details: Plan type, start/end dates, and payment status.
  • Visit History: Logs of when you enter the gym facility (via QR check-in).
  • Payment Information: Transaction history for subscriptions or product purchases. We process payments via secure third-party gateways and do not store full credit card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to deliver the following services:

  • Access Control: Generating digital access passes (QR codes) for gym entry.
  • Membership Management: Processing renewals, upgrades, and cancellations.
  • Communication: Sending push notifications regarding payments, gym announcements, or class schedules.
  • Personalization: Tracking your attendance history to help you stay consistent with your fitness goals.

3. Data Sharing

3.1 Sharing with Your Gym

Your profile, attendance, and payment data are shared with the specific Gym Owner/Facility you are registered with. They require this information to manage their business and ensure your authorized access.

3.2 Third-Party Services

We may share data with:

  • Payment Processors (e.g., Stripe) for secure billing.
  • Cloud Hosting Providers (e.g., Google Firebase) for data storage.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data as long as your membership is active or as needed to provide services. Upon account deletion, identifiers may be anonymized for analytical purposes unless legal requirements mandate retention.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • View your personal profile and attendance history within the app.
  • Request corrections to your personal details.
  • Request the deletion of your account (subject to outstanding payments or contract terms with your gym).
  • Opt-out of non-essential marketing communications.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are generally intended for users aged 16 and above. For users under 18, parental consent is required as per the Gym Owner's specific policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about your data privacy within the app, contact:

Email: support@gymeco.com
